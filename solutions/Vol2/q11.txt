The two rules of struct are:
1. each member must start at an address that is a multiple pf its own size
2. the total size of the struct must be a multiple of its largest member

s1: 2 + 1 + pad(1) + 2 = 6 bytes
s2: 1 + pad(1) + 2 + 4 + 8 = 16 bytes
