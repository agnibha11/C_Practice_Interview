The output is unspecified — commonly 3 2 1 on gcc/x86 (arguments evaluated right to left) but 1 2 3
elsewhere. The C standard does not fix the evaluation ORDER of function arguments, only that each is fully
evaluated before the call. The correct interview answer is to say it is unspecified and that you would
sequence the calls into separate statements. Same family as Volume 1's i++ + i++, but note the
difference: this one is unspecified (one of several orders), not undefined (anything at all).
