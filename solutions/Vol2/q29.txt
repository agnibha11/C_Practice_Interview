Since C99, integer division truncates toward ZERO, and the remainder takes
the sign of the dividend, satisfying (a/b)*b + a%b == a. But arithmetic right shift floors toward negative
infinity, so -7>>1 is -4 while -7/2 is -3. Conclusion for hardware modelling: shifting is NOT the same as
dividing for signed values — a genuine source of mismatches between a C golden model and RTL.
