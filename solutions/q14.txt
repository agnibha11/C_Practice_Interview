the 1 << 31 will make the result as 0x8000000, ans since this is int, C will infer the right shift as arithmatic shift instead of logical.
Hence the final result will be 0xFFFFFFFF, which is 2's complement of -1.

But the next one is unsigned so the right shift will be logical shift and not arithmatic shift, with final result as 0x00000001

The third is again  int, so arithmatic right shift. -8 is 0xFFFFFFF8 in 2's complement, so -8 >> 1 will be
0xfffffffC, which is -4.

-1
1
-4
